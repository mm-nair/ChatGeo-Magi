**Interpretation**

The TMI shows a steady secular‑variation trend of roughly **+30 nT / yr** during this period, consistent with the gradual intensification of the field at the North Atlantic.  This is in line with the predicted secular‑variation from the IGRF‑2025 model, which forecasts a continued increase of about 30 nT per year for the 2025‑2029 decade.
